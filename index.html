Straight answers

The questions people actually ask

What are my chances of winning?

I won’t tell you, because nobody can, and anyone who does is selling. What I promise is a complete, compliant response lodged before the deadline: the thing that has to be true before winning is possible at all. Tenders are decided mostly on price and on whether the buyer is happy with who they’ve already got, and both of those are yours, not mine.

Isn’t a tender alerts subscription enough?

Keep it if you’ve got one, they’re useful. But an alert tells you a tender opened, and by then the site inspection is often booked and the incumbent has known the work was theirs to lose for months. The calendar starts when the contract was signed, not when the buyer announces. And the alert still leaves you with the actual problem, which is that somebody has to write the thing.

What does tender writing cost in Australia?

Tender writing in Australia typically runs $4,000 to $8,000 plus GST per response, or around $165 an hour. OpenTender is fixed and published instead: $3,000 + GST for a council standard tender, back in 5 business days, and $5,000 + GST for a full RFT, back in 10. Panel and whole-of-state work is quoted from $7,500. Nothing is owed until you’ve read a draft and decided you want it finished.

Which industries and regions do you cover?

Any industry with a public contract: cleaning, waste, security, grounds, catering, trades and facilities management have the deepest calendar, and the writing works the same in any sector. Buyers are federal, state and council, Australia wide. If your patch is one state or one region, Watch covers exactly that patch.

Who else have you written for?

Straight answer: OpenTender is new, and you’d be among the first. That’s exactly why the fee is fixed instead of hourly and why the draft comes before the money. You’re not taking a punt on me, you’re reading a document and deciding whether it’s any good. There are no testimonials on this page yet; when there are, they’ll be named, with the client’s permission, or they won’t be here at all.

What happens to our documents?

Your documents are yours. They’re stored in Australia and used for your tenders only: one client’s material never informs another client’s draft. Your pricing, your tender documents and your evidence never go into public tools of any kind, and every claim in a draft traces back to something you gave me or to the tender itself. Ask for your file to be deleted and it’s gone within five business days, with written confirmation.

Do you lodge it for us?

No, unless you ask me to and give me access. Submission is yours, and the declarations are signed by you. What you get from me is the finished response, ready to lodge, ahead of the deadline.
